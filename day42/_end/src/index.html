Kangas that say something.

Every kanga has three parts: the pindo (border), the mji (the centre) and the jina, the saying printed across it. I design all three by hand, then print in small runs on soft cotton.

About me

I grew up watching my grandmother choose kangas in Biashara Street for what they said, not just how they looked.

Today I draw each design in my studio near Fort Jesus, test the colours in the sea light, and print in runs of 50. Every jina is a proverb or a saying I've heard on the coast.
