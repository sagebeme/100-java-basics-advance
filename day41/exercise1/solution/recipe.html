Soft Layered Chapati

Makes 8 chapatis. Takes about an hour, half of it waiting.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of flour
  • 1 teaspoon of salt
  • 1 tablespoon of sugar
  • 1 cup of warm water
  • 3 tablespoons of oil, plus a little for the pan

Method

  1. Mix the flour, salt and sugar in a big bowl.
  2. Add the warm water and oil, and knead for 10 minutes until soft.
  3. Cover and rest for 30 minutes. Don't skip the resting time!
  4. Roll each ball flat, brush with oil, roll it up into a coil, then roll it flat again.
  5. Cook on a hot pan with a little oil until golden on both sides.
